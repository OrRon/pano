Your own Android app doesn't trust it?

Since Android 7, apps only trust user-installed CAs when they opt in. Add this to a debug build:

res/xml/network_security_config.xml
<network-security-config>
  <debug-overrides>
    <trust-anchors>
      <certificates src="user"/>
      <certificates src="system"/>
    </trust-anchors>
  </debug-overrides>
</network-security-config>

AndroidManifest.xml → <application
  android:networkSecurityConfig="@xml/network_security_config">

Debug overrides never apply to release builds.

Apps that pin their certificate will still refuse — that is expected; pano lists them as rejected and you can leave them alone with pano decrypt never.

When you're done

Turn the proxy off in Wi-Fi settings and remove the profile: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › pano CA › Remove.

Turn the proxy off in Wi-Fi settings and remove the certificate: Encryption & credentials › Trusted credentials › User › pano.

Turn the proxy off and remove the certificate from the trust store.

Also reachable as http://pano.internal from any device already proxied through pano.
